Reading a variable's sensitivities from a full-state solution evaluates the chain rule over blocks of time points in compiled code, reusing it across solves of the same model, instead of building a Jacobian per time point in Python. A DFN voltage over 3001 time points now takes 4 ms instead of 0.3 s, and over a 120-step experiment 12 ms instead of 0.85 s. An `IDAKLUSolver` solution also stores its state sensitivities once, each parameter's entry a view of the `"all"` array, halving their memory.
