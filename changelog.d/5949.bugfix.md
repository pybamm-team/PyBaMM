For a DAE whose mass matrix is the identity, with the default `newton_mode`, `IDAKLUSolver` sensitivities are consistent at the initial time, also with `calc_ic=False`, and after every `t_eval` breakpoint, as the next `pybammsolvers` release solves the linear sensitivity equations directly instead of leaving them to `IDACalcIC`, whose ignored failures could leave a DFN's `dV/dI(t=0) = 0`. With `init_all_y_ic=True`, they no longer depend on what an earlier solve left in IDAS.
