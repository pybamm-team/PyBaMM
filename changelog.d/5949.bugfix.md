`IDAKLUSolver` sensitivities of a standard-form DAE are consistent at the initial time, also with `calc_ic=False`, and after every `t_eval` breakpoint, with the default `newton_mode`. The next `pybammsolvers` release solves for them directly instead of leaving them to `IDACalcIC`, whose ignored failures could give a DFN `dV/dI(t=0) = 0`. With `init_all_y_ic=True`, sensitivities no longer depend on what an earlier solve left in IDAS.
