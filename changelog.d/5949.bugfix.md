`IDAKLUSolver` sensitivities are now consistent at the initial time and after every `t_eval` breakpoint. `pybammsolvers` solves the linear sensitivity equations for the algebraic sensitivities and the sensitivity time derivatives directly, instead of relying on `IDACalcIC`, whose failures at tight tolerances (e.g. DFN with Ai2020 at `atol=1e-11`) were ignored and left `dV/dI(t=0) = 0`. This also fixes the t0 sensitivities with `calc_ic=False` and with a non-identity mass matrix, and every sensitivity with `init_all_y_ic=True`, and lets sensitivity solves that failed with `IDA_ERR_FAIL` (e.g. DFN with Ecker2015 at `rtol=1e-9`) succeed. Sensitivity initial conditions that cannot be found now raise a `SolverError`.
