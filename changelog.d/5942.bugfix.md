A concatenation of `FiniteVolumeUnstructured` domains stacked in x, paired by `pybamm.Mesh`, now gives each child its outer `"top"`/`"bottom"` (and `"front"`/`"back"`) boundary conditions. Before, the children got only `"left"`/`"right"`, so an equation on one child, such as `div(grad(c_n))`, treated those sides as zero flux: for u = z its residual was 3 instead of 0. Equations on the concatenation itself, as in `BasicDFNUnstructured`, are unchanged.
