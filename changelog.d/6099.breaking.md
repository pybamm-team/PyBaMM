Renamed the lumped thermal heat-flow variables and made each positive in the direction it names: `"Surface total cooling [W]"` and `"[W.m-3]"` are now `"Core to surface heat flux [W]"` and `"[W.m-3]"` (positive out of the core), and `"Environment total cooling [W]"` is now `"Surface to environment heat flux [W]"` (positive into the environment). With an ambient surface, `"Surface to environment heat flux [W]"` now equals the core-to-surface heat flux for lumped models instead of 0, and is no longer defined for the distributed pouch-cell and x-full thermal models, which never computed it. Migration: use the new names and flip the sign.
