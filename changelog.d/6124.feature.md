A model whose discretisation defaults hold a prebuilt `UnstructuredSubMesh` now round-trips through `Serialise.serialise_custom_model` and `Serialise.load_custom_model`: `UnstructuredSubMesh` serialises its vertices, elements, coordinate system and boundary faces, interface data is recomputed on load, and `pybamm.Mesh` matches a string `var_pts` key to a custom spatial variable of that name.
