In a half cell, a scalar `"open-circuit potential"` or `"intercalation kinetics"` value that needs a host lattice (a hysteresis or `"MSMR"` OCP, `"coupled ion-electron transfer"` or `"MSMR"` kinetics) now applies to the working electrode only, so these half cells and `pybamm.lithium_ion.MSMR` half cells build instead of failing; giving such a value to the lithium metal electrode explicitly raises an `OptionError`. Fixes [#6093](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/issues/6093).
