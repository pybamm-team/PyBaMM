Coupled variables are now resolved before parameter processing, so a `CoupledVariable` whose target holds a `Parameter`, or whose variable has a parameter-dependent `scale` or `reference`, solves instead of failing. Fixes [#6126](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/issues/6126).
- Coupled variables inside output variables are resolved when variables are processed eagerly, and inside the flux of `("Flux", ...)` boundary conditions.
- `Discretisation(remove_independent_variables_from_rhs=True)` now sees dependencies hidden behind coupled variables.
- A coupled variable whose target is missing now raises during `ParameterValues.process_model` instead of during discretisation, and a circular chain of coupled variables raises a `ModelError` instead of a `RecursionError`.
